osc 52

OSC 52 allows remote terminal applications (like vim/tmux) to copy text directly to your local system clipboard. Popular compatible terminals include iTerm2, Kitty, Alacritty, WezTerm, Windows Terminal, and Foot. It works seamlessly over SSH without requiring X11 forwarding.
